## Purpose

Support local-only and federated communities with independent access policies while storing each post once and preserving site and community decisions.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared object identity
The system SHALL store one logical post and one current normalized body per canonical ActivityPub object URI in a database, or per stable local identity for non-federated posts. Concurrent imports across sites SHALL converge on that object without granting access. Different identities SHALL remain distinct even when their content matches. Authoritative updates SHALL preserve identity, and an unrelated sender SHALL NOT replace a stored object by reusing its URI.

#### Scenario: Concurrent reception at two sites
- **WHEN** sites A and B receive the same object URI concurrently
- **THEN** one shared post is stored and each site's accepted reception is recorded separately

#### Scenario: Identical content with different identities
- **WHEN** two independently authored objects contain identical text but have different URIs
- **THEN** they remain separate posts

### Requirement: Access-controlled forum operations
Authenticated accounts SHALL be able to create local-only or federated communities at their own site and choose one of the three supported read/post modes. Eligible actors SHALL be able to publish titled topics and nested replies, subscribe and unsubscribe where supported, edit their own posts, and delete their own posts subject to community access controls. Authorized readers SHALL be able to retrieve paginated community and discussion listings. Community creation SHALL record a creator and initial management authority; local-only members and managers SHALL belong to the owning site. Categories SHALL NOT be required by these operations.

#### Scenario: Publish a topic and nested reply
- **WHEN** an eligible account publishes a topic and another eligible account replies to a reply
- **THEN** the system retains the topic title, author identities, community association, and direct parent relationships

#### Scenario: Unauthorized edit
- **WHEN** an account attempts to edit another author's post without author authority
- **THEN** the mutation is rejected and the shared body is unchanged

### Requirement: Local-only operation without federation
Local-only communities SHALL support authorized topics, replies, reactions, editing, deletion, membership, and management through local APIs without generating ActivityPub activities or remote synchronization. Local-only and federation-participation settings SHALL be fixed at creation in this change; read/post policy SHALL remain independently manageable. Local-only resources SHALL be excluded from public listings, federated import, user outboxes, object sharing, and cross-site reception.

#### Scenario: Site-wide local community remains non-federated
- **WHEN** an authenticated site user posts in a local-only community open to all owning-site users
- **THEN** other eligible site users can access the post but no ActivityPub representation, activity, or delivery is created

#### Scenario: Local-only community conversion requested
- **WHEN** a manager attempts to change a local-only community into a federated community
- **THEN** the operation is rejected without exporting existing content

### Requirement: No access-boundary bypass through relationships
Topics, replies, contexts, reactions, links generated by the server, and their representations SHALL inherit the hosting community's read policy. Automatic sharing, moving, replying, or importing across incompatible local-only or member-readable boundaries SHALL be rejected if it exposes restricted identity or content. Authorized reads SHALL not populate a public shared cache, and unauthorized results SHALL not reveal restricted metadata or counts.

#### Scenario: Public share of a restricted object
- **WHEN** an actor attempts to share a local-only or member-readable post into a public outbox or community
- **THEN** the operation is rejected without exposing the original object's identity or content

#### Scenario: Shared restricted object at another receiving site
- **WHEN** a non-member requests a member-readable federated post already stored by another site
- **THEN** shared storage and receipt presence do not permit that non-member to read it

### Requirement: Independent reception and community inclusion
Post existence, site reception, declared community or context association, and authoritative community or context inclusion SHALL be distinguishable. A locally created post targeting a remote community SHALL retain a pending or rejected inclusion state until acceptance is established, without preventing access to the author's own post.

#### Scenario: Remote community rejects a submission
- **WHEN** an account submits a topic to a remote community that rejects it
- **THEN** the topic is not shown as accepted community content and the submitting account can observe the rejection

### Requirement: Author deletion and scoped moderation
Verified author deletion SHALL mark the shared object deleted and remove its body from normal presentation across receiving sites, retaining a tombstone against replay. Site hiding and community moderation removal SHALL affect their own scope without impersonating the author or deleting unrelated site or community copies of the shared object's presentation.

#### Scenario: Replay after author deletion
- **WHEN** an old Create arrives after verified deletion of that object
- **THEN** the deleted body is not restored

#### Scenario: Community removal
- **WHEN** an authorized moderator removes a post from one community
- **THEN** that community excludes it without converting the action into global author deletion

### Requirement: Safe content and basic interactions
The system SHALL render local and remote textual content through a sanitization boundary and support likes and undoing likes on topics and replies, preserving reacting actor identity. Unsupported extensions SHALL NOT grant permissions or break supported text and reply processing.

#### Scenario: Unsafe remote HTML
- **WHEN** an otherwise valid remote post contains executable HTML
- **THEN** the displayed content cannot execute that HTML

#### Scenario: Undo a like
- **WHEN** the same actor undoes an existing like
- **THEN** its reaction is removed without affecting another actor's reaction
